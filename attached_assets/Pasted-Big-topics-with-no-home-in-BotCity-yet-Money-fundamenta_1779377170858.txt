Curricular structure suggestion
A real financial-ed game has a progression: earn → budget → save → borrow wisely → invest → protect → plan → give. Your city is mostly tax-flavored end-state knowledge. Adding the foundational layer (budget, savings, credit, behavioral) would let a 16-year-old start the game and actually learn finance from zero.
